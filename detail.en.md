DSH HM Console (free build): a pairing panel inside DSH settings. Scan the QR code (or type the 6-digit code) to connect your phone to this computer's DSH — then send messages, browse history, transfer files and view the screen from the phone app. Public access needs no membership or payment: either point it at your own server (self-hosted frps) or turn on the built-in free tunnel (no signup, random public URL).
